## 2. New research risk identified by the source audit

### 2.1 Originality and construct overlap
Three antecedents must be compared explicitly **before** claiming novelty:

- **SMART (2019):** clinically anchored software-assisted hazards, risk and safety-case traceability.
- **CREOLA (2025):** structured clinician judgement of LLM output errors and potential harm, with human adjudication.
- **2018 Habli safety-case study:** empirical analysis of what makes existing clinical safety arguments difficult to evaluate.

**Proposed differentiating hypothesis (untested):** Given a *submitted*, dated clinical AI deployment decision and its evidence, an independent scenario-aware challenge detects clinically consequential omissions, flawed control reasoning or unsupported risk/governance conclusions missed by a prespecified conventional CSO review at an acceptable false-warning burden.

The hypothesis is **not** established by the above studies; comparison and evaluation are still needed.

### 2.2 The proper comparison is not 'dangerous vs safe AI'
Several AVT sources report positive user/workload outcomes and many well-formed draft notes, while also identifying nontrivial error and integration concerns. A conservative decision-challenge engine should acknowledge a control that is convincingly verified; it must **not** warn merely because an external paper says some other scribe made an error.

### 2.3 Risks in our own study design
- **Construct mismatch:** output error rates ≠ local control effectiveness ≠ hazard-log completeness ≠ clinically consequential harm.
- **Hindsight leakage:** 2026 findings cannot be used to accuse a 2024 or 2025 original deployment decision of ignoring them.
- **False-positive burden:** generic lists of harms could overwhelm CSO workflows.
- **Researcher contamination:** authors who designed the Oxford synthetic case and seven patterns cannot supply the gold-standard hazard labels.
- **Reviewer independence:** source-methodology reviewers must be separate from blinded clinical case reviewers.
- **Competing interest:** record supplier funding and author employment in primary studies; do not discount work solely for industry participation but address potential bias.

## 3. Appraisal and evidence status

This is **a structured preliminary critical appraisal**, not validated GRADE evidence, Cochrane RoB2, ROBINS-I, CASP or a PRISMA systematic review. Those instruments may be applied where suitable to studies addressing a well-defined inference; assigning a single numeric 'quality score' across methods, standards and empirical studies would be misleading.
